CohortShift comparison

Methodology: Shares are the stratum count divided by that population's denominator. The marginal gap is the study share minus the target share, in percentage points. For an intersection, the feasible count is at least max(0, count A + count B - N) and at most min(count A, count B). The gap bounds subtract opposite endpoints. An exact joint count replaces those bounds only when every joint cell is observed and the cells sum to both declared margins. These figures describe cohort composition. They are not a treatment effect, not an imputed outcome, and not evidence that a result generalizes.

Limitations:
- Aggregate counts only. Individual records are rejected.
- An observed zero is a source count of zero. Suppressed, absent, and unevaluable cells are not zeros.
- A bounded intersection is the set of counts consistent with the margins. It is not an observed gap.
- Equal marginal shares do not imply an equal intersection.
- No clinical recommendation and no claim of treatment generalizability is made.
